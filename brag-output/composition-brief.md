## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: Quiet premium engineering launch film — sharp, brutalist, confident, zero fluff.
- Interpretation: Crisp ease-out reveals, high typography scale contrast, stark color discipline (dark neutral + electric lime accent). No purple gradients or cartoon fluff.
- Angle: Modernist, reliable software engineering built on real data integrity.
- Hook: The colossal 2-line Anton typography slams in over a responsive coordinate grid.
- Outro / punchline: Clean terminal contact lockup with live domain and email.
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS buzzwords
  - Abstract spinning shapes or 3D spheres
  - Any purple/violet hues
